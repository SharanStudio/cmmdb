A friendly community of colleagues

Community Medicine Malayalis (CMM) is an informal, volunteer-run, friendly community for MD Community Medicine professionals and trainees with roots in Kerala.

CMM has two major initiatives to date:

  1. The WhatsApp Community
  2. CMM Online
CMM is not a professional body. We have no membership fees, no office-bearers, and no rulebook.

Vision

To create a world where every community medicine colleague with their roots in Kerala knows each other, learns from each other, and looks out for each other.

Mission

We connect professionals with an MD in Community Medicine and trainees pursuing the MD degree in Community Medicine with roots in Kerala, wherever they work in the world, so they can share knowledge, opportunities, and support, with no hierarchy and no barriers.

The WhatsApp Community

Dr Amith E S started this community as a small, informal initiative, and Dr Amal Kareem’s work on day-to-day operations helped it grow. Many other colleagues have also helped to keep the discussions going and bring in new members.

We share upcoming webinars, workshops, and conferences organised by professional associations, medical colleges and research organisations. We swap experience, ask questions, and look after each other along the way. The community has helped many aspirants to make informed PG choices and career decisions in the past.

A small volunteer admin team verifies each new member, so the group stays limited to colleagues. Beyond that, everyone can talk and share.

If you are a Community Medicine professional (MD in Community Medicine) or trainee, you can request to join the CMM Community on WhatsApp.

CMM Online

When the CMM group became big, the admin team created CMM Online, a website that helps you find each other faster. It is a searchable listing of members. Use it to find a colleague by name, institution, or place, and reach out.

Your data

CMM Online lists only what members choose to share. We never publish mobile numbers. Your email appears only if you say Yes. You can ask for your profile to be removed at any time.
