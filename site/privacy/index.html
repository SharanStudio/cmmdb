Privacy notice

Notice version: 7 November 2026  ·  About CMM

Who we are

CMM Online at cmmonline.in is the public directory of Community Medicine Malayalis (CMM). The CMM Online core group, a group of community medicine colleagues, runs it.

The core group decides what personal data the Directory collects and why. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the core group is therefore the data fiduciary. The data belongs to the members who provide it, and no individual owns it.

Contact and grievance address: admincmmonline@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you submit the registration Form, we collect:

  • Your Google account email address, verified by Google.
  • Your full name, mobile number, secondary mobile number (if given) and contact email.
  • Your native district in Kerala.
  • Your UG and PG college, state and year; your professional status, designation, organization and working state or district.
  • Your areas of interest.
  • Your consent, your choice about showing your email, and the time you submitted.

What we publish, and what we keep private

Published on cmmonline.inNever published
Name, native district, UG and PG college and state, years, professional status, designation, organization, areas of interest, and your contact email only if you chose to show it. Mobile numbers (primary and secondary), your Google login email, your consent record and your submission time.

Anyone on the internet can read the published profile. Search engines, scrapers and other automated tools can copy public pages, so share only what you are comfortable with.

Why we use your data

  • To list you in the public directory, so fellow members can find batchmates, seniors, juniors and collaborators.
  • To prevent duplicate and fake entries. We use your mobile number and your Google login email only for this, and to check that a change or removal request comes from you.
  • To contact you about your entry, for example to confirm a change or a removal.
  • To check that a new registration is really you. If someone registers with a mobile number that is already in the directory under a different Google account, we email the existing account. The email shows the new registration’s name, UG college and a partly hidden email address, with two buttons: “Yes, it’s me” and “No, it’s not me”. The buttons work for 7 days. If you answer Yes, we keep one profile. If you answer No, we hold the new registration for review. We email you to confirm we received your answer, and you can write to us to change it.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box on the Form.

Who else handles your data

  • Google stores the Form responses and the Sheet, runs our scripts, sends our emails and receives your answer on the confirmation page (Google Forms, Sheets, Apps Script and Gmail). The confirmation page at cmmonline.in/confirm sends your answer to a Google Apps Script service and sets no cookies.
  • GitHub stores and serves the published directory file. GitHub also keeps earlier versions of that file in its version history.
  • GoatCounter gives us anonymous, cookie-free visit counts for this website.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your entry while you remain listed. If you ask us to remove it, we delete it from the Sheet and the website within 7 days. Earlier copies may remain in GitHub’s version history, and we delete those on request where we can. We cannot recall copies that other people have already made from the public page.

Your rights and how to use them

  • Correct or update your entry: sign in with the same Google account and submit the Form again.
  • Withdraw consent and remove your entry: use the “Remove my profile” link in the footer of cmmonline.in, or email admincmmonline@gmail.com, with your full name and the email address you registered with. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent. We may ask you to confirm from the registered account. We act within 7 days.
  • Ask what we hold about you: use the same address.

Complaints

Write to us first at admincmmonline@gmail.com. We respond within 7 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who this directory is for

This directory is for adult medical professionals. We do not knowingly list anyone under 18.

Changes to this notice

When we change this notice, we update the version date at the top of this page and on the registration Form.
